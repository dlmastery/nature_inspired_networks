# Abstract — A Self-Auditing LLM-Agent Autoresearch Protocol That Catches Its Own Headline Drift

We present a self-auditing LLM-agent autoresearch protocol that catches its own headline drift. On a 62-hypothesis third-party-code calibration substrate (`pytorch/vision`, `timm`, HuggingFace Transformers, Lightning Bolts, `torch.optim`, `state-spaces/mamba`), the protocol's implementation-critic tier registers **0 MAJOR/BROKEN findings**; on its own 84-hypothesis nature-inspired-priors substrate, it registers **22 pp MAJOR/BROKEN excess** (Fisher exact two-sided **p = 1.94 × 10⁻⁵**; Wilson 95% CIs non-overlapping). The protocol's double-barrelled load-bearing existence proof is (i) it caught **H09 phi_budget's 12.6% realised-stage-ratio drift** via Fixer-introduced mechanism-pinning tests (commit `519cdf3`), and (ii) it caught its OWN headline-prior fragility: three default-config-certified Phase-8 winners (`pair_gm_pdw`, `slot_act_sine`, `sg_only_phi_budget`) clear Holm-Bonferroni α'=0.0167 at matched-recipe n=7 (paired Wilcoxon p=0.0078 each) but do NOT survive a properly LR-tuned baseline (tuned baseline beats all three by +2.27 to +2.81 pp at n=3; Mann-Whitney p_one ∈ [0.0083, 0.0111]). The default-config matched-recipe certification stands as a **secondary** empirical claim. We honestly demote the priors to "screened candidates that survive matched-recipe certification but not iso-tuned-LR" and elevate the protocol-as-meta-research-methodology as the headline contribution.
