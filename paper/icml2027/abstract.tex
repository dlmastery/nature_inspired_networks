\begin{abstract}
We present a self-auditing LLM-agent autoresearch protocol that catches
\emph{and corrects} its own headline-interpretation drift. On a
62-hypothesis third-party-code calibration substrate
(\texttt{pytorch/vision}, \texttt{timm}, HuggingFace Transformers,
Lightning Bolts, \texttt{torch.optim}, \texttt{state-spaces/mamba}),
the protocol's implementation-critic tier registers \textbf{0
MAJOR/BROKEN findings}; on its own 84-hypothesis
nature-inspired-priors substrate, it registers \textbf{22\,pp
MAJOR/BROKEN excess} (Fisher exact two-sided $p = 1.94 \times 10^{-5}$;
Wilson 95\% CIs non-overlapping). The protocol's double-barrelled
existence proof is (i)~it caught \emph{H09 phi\_budget's 12.6\%
realised-stage-ratio drift} via Fixer-introduced mechanism-pinning
tests (commit \texttt{519cdf3}), and (ii)~it caught \emph{and then
corrected} its own headline-interpretation drift across recipes: three
default-config-certified Phase-8 winners (\texttt{pair\_gm\_pdw},
\texttt{slot\_act\_sine}, \texttt{sg\_only\_phi\_budget}) clear
Holm--Bonferroni $\alpha'{=}0.0167$ at matched-recipe $n{=}7$ (paired
Wilcoxon $p{=}0.0078$ each); a Phase-9h $n{=}3$ tuned-baseline-at-
lr$=$0.01 diagnostic \emph{appeared} to refute them (tuned baseline
beats all three by $+2.27$ to $+2.81$\,pp; Mann--Whitney
$p_{\mathrm{one}} \in [0.0083, 0.0111]$) -- but that comparison is
apples-to-oranges (asymmetric LR sweep across different (lr, wd)
cells). At iso-modern-recipe (11-trick recipe) and iso-convergence
(200\,ep) $n{=}3$, all three priors \emph{lift} the modern convergent
baseline (mean $0.6360$) by $+1.00$ to $+1.24$\,pp with $3/3$ positive
paired deltas and Phase-5 ordinal-gate PASS for all three (paired
Wilcoxon hits the $n{=}3$ floor $p_{\mathrm{one}}{=}0.125$; formal
NeurIPS-$\alpha$ cert at iso-modern-recipe requires $n{\geq}7$, filed
as Phase-9j future work). The priors carry $\sim{+1}$\,pp of robust
directional signal across both default-config and modern-recipe
regimes; the Phase-9h gap is correctly localised to LR-tuning
confound, not prior failure. We retain the methodology --- self-
auditing protocol with iso-recipe / iso-convergence guardrails --- as
the headline contribution, and restore the priors to ``screened
candidates with consistent $+1$\,pp directional lift across recipes;
formal iso-modern-recipe cert pending $n{\geq}7$.''
\end{abstract}
