\begin{abstract}
We present a self-auditing LLM-agent autoresearch protocol that catches
its own headline drift. On a 62-hypothesis third-party-code calibration
substrate (\texttt{pytorch/vision}, \texttt{timm}, HuggingFace
Transformers, Lightning Bolts, \texttt{torch.optim},
\texttt{state-spaces/mamba}), the protocol's implementation-critic tier
registers \textbf{0 MAJOR/BROKEN findings}; on its own 84-hypothesis
nature-inspired-priors substrate, it registers \textbf{22\,pp
MAJOR/BROKEN excess} (Fisher exact two-sided $p = 1.94 \times 10^{-5}$;
Wilson 95\% CIs non-overlapping). The protocol's double-barrelled
load-bearing existence proof is (i)~it caught \emph{H09 phi\_budget's
12.6\% realised-stage-ratio drift} via Fixer-introduced
mechanism-pinning tests (commit \texttt{519cdf3}), and (ii)~it caught
its OWN headline-prior fragility: three default-config-certified
Phase-8 winners (\texttt{pair\_gm\_pdw}, \texttt{slot\_act\_sine},
\texttt{sg\_only\_phi\_budget}) clear Holm--Bonferroni
$\alpha'{=}0.0167$ at matched-recipe $n{=}7$ (paired Wilcoxon
$p{=}0.0078$ each) but do NOT survive a properly LR-tuned baseline
(tuned baseline beats all three by $+2.27$ to $+2.81$\,pp at $n{=}3$;
Mann--Whitney $p_{\mathrm{one}} \in [0.0083, 0.0111]$). The
default-config matched-recipe certification stands as a
\emph{secondary} empirical claim. We honestly demote the priors to
``screened candidates that survive matched-recipe certification but
not iso-tuned-LR'' and elevate the protocol-as-meta-research-methodology
as the headline contribution.
\end{abstract}
